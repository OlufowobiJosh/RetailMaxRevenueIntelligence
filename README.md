# RetailMax | Revenue Intelligence & Customer Profitability

**Power BI · DAX · Data Modelling · Retail Analytics**

A retail analytics case study developed as part of my Amdari project work. This four-page Power BI report explores revenue, gross profit, customer segments, product performance, sales channels and discount patterns to support commercial analysis.

![RetailMax Executive Revenue Overview](dashboards/01_Executive_Revenue_Overview.png)

## Business problem

RetailMax needs to understand how sales performance translates into profitability across its customers, products, channels and regions. The analysis explores where revenue and profit are concentrated, how customer groups compare, and which pricing and discount patterns warrant further investigation.

## Business solution

Developed a four-page Power BI report bringing revenue, profitability, customer, product and discount analysis into one reporting tool. Created DAX measures and visual summaries to help managers compare commercial performance and identify areas for investigation.

The report is designed to support reviews of pricing, product mix, customer profitability and sales-channel performance.

## Tools and approach

- **Power BI Desktop:** report development and visualisation.
- **DAX:** measures for revenue, profit, customers and discounts.
- **Data modelling:** relationships between sales transactions and customer, product, store and date information.
- **Analysis:** comparisons across customer segments, categories, brands, channels, regions and the displayed monthly profile.

## Key findings

Observations from the dashboard views include:

1. **Online leads the channel comparison**, with the highest displayed revenue and gross profit.
2. **Home Appliances leads category revenue**, followed by Lifestyle in the displayed comparison.
3. **West leads the regional gross profit comparison**, followed by North.
4. **Loyalty-member and non-member revenue appear closely matched**, providing a starting point for further customer-value analysis.

## Business recommendations

- Assess channel profitability alongside fulfilment, marketing and service costs before allocating additional resources.
- Review product categories using both revenue and margin when considering stock and promotion priorities.
- Compare customer-segment profitability and purchasing behaviour to inform targeted campaign planning.
- Evaluate discounting alongside product mix and profit margin when reviewing promotion performance.
